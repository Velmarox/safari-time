SAFARITIME - CHANGELOG
Versioning scheme: v#.#.#-[stage] (semver)
Patch = fixes | Minor = new content (patch resets) | Major 0 until launch (v1.0.0)
Stages: -alpha -> -beta at feature-complete -> -rc1 when launch-ready -> v1.0.0 gold
A bump renames the build root folder and adds an entry here; working files keep their names.
Archived (outgoing-version) copies live in _version_archive/, never in the build folder.

v0.3.0-alpha (2026-09-08)
- Rules (from the IGT "The Wild Life" reference): triggering Free Games also pays a bonus prize
  equal to the total bet times the number of free games awarded (10x / 15x / 20x total bet).
  Scatter RTP is now 6.5 points; line paytable scaled down ~x0.93 to hold the total near 92%.
- Strips: a Wild is never laid within two stops of an Africa, so the two can never show on the
  same reel (a Wild does not substitute for Africa). Enforced by assertion and a test.
- Presentation: reels roll and land one at a time with a pause between landings; the PLAY button
  becomes STOP while they roll and lands the rest immediately. Extra-long roll once two Africa
  are showing. The Africa hit is a new brass sample that rises a whole step on each successive
  Africa, and stays silent once the bonus can no longer be reached on the remaining reels.
- Art: new Africa symbol (blue/silver medallion) replaces the gold one so it stands out.
- 03_Assets_Audio/Africa_Symbol.wav replaced with the new sample.
  (archived to _version_archive/SafariTime_Docs_v0.2.0-alpha/03_Assets_Audio/Africa_Symbol.wav,
   .../01_Design_Core/Game_Overview.md, .../02_Math_Model/Math_Spec.md, .../02_Math_Model/Par_Sheet.md,
   .../CHANGELOG.txt)

v0.2.0-alpha (2026-09-08)
- Maths: WILD removed from reels 1 and 5 in the base game (WILD_REELS = (1, 2, 3); it was already
  absent there in Free Games). Base line RTP fell 75.4% -> 61.5%, so the paytable was rescaled
  x1.17 (e.g. Lion x5 1250 -> 1500) to restore a ~92% total. Par_Sheet.md regenerated; Math_Spec.md
  and Game_Overview.md updated. (archived to _version_archive/SafariTime_Docs_v0.1.1-alpha/02_Math_Model/
  Math_Spec.md and Par_Sheet.md)
- New 03_Assets_Audio/: 15 source WAVs supplied by the user (2 looping BGMs, 13 one-shot
  effects), renamed to Snake_Case from their original spaced names.
- Web build: WAVs encoded to MP3 by engine/encode_audio.js into web/assets/audio/; the game
  now has music (base / free games loops), reel, win, animal, scatter and bonus sounds, and a
  mute toggle. Game_Overview.md presentation section updated.
  (archived to _version_archive/SafariTime_Docs_v0.1.1-alpha/CHANGELOG.txt)
